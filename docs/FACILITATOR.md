# Facilitator crib: Trust in Balance

**Time:** about 45 minutes for 3–6 students: 5 to choose roles, about 8 per quarter, 8 for the year-end.
These timings come from a simulated session. Replace them with your own after a real one.

**Before:** open `index.html` on the projected laptop, press F11, then click **Facilitator** in the top bar. Each quarter now shows your prompt and a timer. Print the role cards from **Facilitator notes → Print role cards**.

**Roles:** one card per student. One person drives the mouse; everyone argues. With fewer than six players, leave roles out rather than doubling up.

| Quarter | Ask | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 Infection outbreak (9 min) | CPO and CFO: agency nurses protect tired staff but cost twice as much. Who wins this quarter, and why? | If they pick agency, point at "Already agreed" in the board pack next quarter. If they don't, ask the MD what happens to safety. |
| Q2 No beds (8 min) | COO: are we short of beds, staff, or a way home? Point to one piece of evidence. | Extra beds look obvious. Ask "who staffs them?", then show the knock-on step in the replay. |
| Q3 In the news (8 min) | CEO: what can we say today that we will still stand behind in three months? | Facing the cameras is popular. Ask what it commits the board to deliver next quarter. |
| Q4 Winter surge (8 min) | Everyone: which of your own goals would you give up to protect someone else's? | Check the money red line before they vote. This is where it usually breaks. |

**Each lock-in:** ask the group to agree the biggest hit and the reviewer out loud before tapping. The reviewer should be the role that looks after that measure.

**Running late?** In the replay, press **S** (or Skip). Leave "What if we'd chosen…" for the year-end.

**Keys for the driver:** A–D pick a proposal · Enter lock it in · in the lock-in 1–5 pick the bar, Q–Y the reviewer, Enter confirm · → next replay step · ← back · S skip · N or Enter on to the next quarter · F facilitator mode.

**From the end of Q2:** the **On this course** stamp gives the rating the inspectors would give if nothing else changed, counting costs already coming next quarter. Use it as a warning light, not a scoreboard: if it says a red line goes next quarter, ask who has a plan.

**Year-end (8 min):** each role names one decision that hurt their goals. Was it worth it for the hospital? What evidence would have changed the vote? Then run the four-question learning check together.

**Three red lines (for everyone):** safety never below 55; access never below 35; deficit never beyond £5.0m.

**Words students may ask about:** deficit (spending more than you receive), agency staff (temporary, at a higher rate), discharge (going home), virtual ward (recovering at home with remote checks), ongoing cost (a cost that comes back every quarter). The in-game **Word list** has them all.
